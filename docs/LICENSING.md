# Using and sharing Grepleaks

Original Grepleaks contributions use the
[Grepleaks Source Available License 1.0](../LICENSE). The source can be read,
modified and shared without charge. Professional use is allowed; selling the
software or paid access to it requires separate permission.

This page explains the intended distinction. The license text governs.

| Activity | Under this license |
| :--- | :--- |
| Use Grepleaks for learning, research or your team's internal security work | Allowed. |
| Use it to perform paid pentests or audits for clients | Allowed. |
| Charge for reports, findings, consulting or training | Allowed, without selling the software or hosted access. |
| Charge for installation or support work | Allowed when the fee is for that work rather than a software copy, license or hosted access. |
| Modify Grepleaks and share a free fork | Allowed; keep applicable notices and the restrictions on Grepleaks material. |
| Copy Grepleaks, rename it and sell it | Requires separate written permission. |
| Sell a modified Grepleaks fork or bundle it as part of paid software | Requires separate written permission. |
| Sell hosted access, a subscription or an API exposing Grepleaks as a software service | Requires separate written permission. |
| Reuse independently obtained OpenCode or another third-party component | Governed by that component's own license. |

## What the restriction covers

The restriction covers original, copyrightable Grepleaks material and derivatives
that incorporate it. It does not give Grepleaks ownership of OpenCode, external
tools, facts, general ideas or third-party skills. Their existing license rights
remain available. The repository's [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
and [engine licensing notice](../engine/GREPLEAKS_LICENSE.md) explain the split.

Your reports and findings do not automatically become Grepleaks-licensed. If an
output includes licensed code or other incorporated material, that material keeps
its applicable terms.

## Source available

Because the license restricts resale and paid software access, Grepleaks is
**source available**, rather than open source under the
[Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd). Its underlying open-source
components remain under their original licenses.

## Maintainer review before publication

This is a custom license prepared to express the project's requested policy;
it is not a standardized or OSI-approved license, nor a legal opinion about
its enforceability. Have a qualified lawyer review the wording, the ownership
and provenance of the covered contributions, and the boundary between paid
professional work and paid software access before publishing.

Any separate commercial permission must come from the holders of the relevant
rights. This change does not revoke rights under copies previously supplied
under MIT or another license, if any were distributed that way.
